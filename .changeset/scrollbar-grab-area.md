Scrollbars in every component keep their thin look but can now be grabbed across
a 10px strip, and the thumb widens while hovered. `tk-table` gets a
`scrollbarSize` prop (`thin` | `base` | `large`); the `--tk-scrollbar-size`,
`--tk-scrollbar-inset` and `--tk-scrollbar-hover-inset` custom properties tune
all components at once.
